About us

Your locksmith for every season.

All Seasons Locksmith is a fully mobile, insured locksmith based in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania. We built this company on one idea: fast, honest lock and key help you can count on any day of the year — for your car, your home, and your business.

Our technicians work locally across Dauphin, Cumberland and Lebanon counties in fully-stocked vans, so most jobs are handled on-site in a single visit — with non-destructive entry and a clear quote before we start. Our Harrisburg shop welcomes in-store visits by appointment.

Good to know

Questions we
hear a lot.

Still unsure? Call (223) 240-3505 — a real person picks up.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Harrisburg and serve all of Dauphin, Cumberland, and Lebanon counties in Central Pennsylvania — including Carlisle, Mechanicsburg, Hershey, Lebanon, and the surrounding towns.

How fast can you get to me?

As quickly as we can. Our technicians work locally in fully-stocked mobile vans, so we head your way right after your call — we'll give you a time estimate when we speak, based on where you are and how busy the day is.

Can you make car keys without going to a dealership?

In most cases, yes. We cut and program replacement keys, transponder keys, and key fobs on-site for a wide range of makes and models — often faster than a dealership.

Do you install smart locks and keyless entry?

Yes. We install and set up smart locks for homes and keyless-entry / access-control systems for businesses, and we'll help you pick the right option.

Can I visit your shop?

Our Harrisburg location handles in-store visits by appointment only, so we can give you our full attention — just call ahead and we'll set a time.

How much does a service cost?

Every job is a little different, so we give you a clear, upfront quote before we begin — no surprises. The fastest way to get a number is to call us and tell us what's going on.
