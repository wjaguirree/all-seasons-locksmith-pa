Good to know

Common questions.

What should I have ready for lost car key replacement in Highspire?

Have your vehicle registration, photo ID, and the exact make, model, and year ready before the visit. If you still have a broken blade, a dead fob, or a valet key, keep that nearby too. Those details help us choose the correct blank, program the right chip, and avoid a second trip.

Can you make a replacement key if I have no keys at all?

Yes. If there are no keys left, the job usually moves to key origination. That can involve VIN-based lookup, decoding a lock, and then cutting and programming a new key on-site. The exact steps depend on the vehicle's security system, whether it uses a transponder, and whether the locks are worn or aftermarket.

Do modern car keys need programming, or is cutting enough?

Most newer vehicles need both. A metal blade may open the door, but the engine may still stay disabled until the transponder or smart key is programmed to the immobilizer. Many Toyota, Honda, Ford, Chevrolet, Nissan, Hyundai, Kia, Jeep, RAM, Subaru, and BMW models fall into that category.

What makes a lost car key job harder than a simple duplicate?

No original key, a damaged ignition, a worn door lock, or an aftermarket alarm can make the work more involved. Laser-cut and smart/proximity keys also require the right equipment and programming steps. If the vehicle has had prior lock changes, decoding or VIN origination may take extra checking before we cut anything.

Yes. We handle lockout calls in Highspire, including the riverfront blocks and the Second Street area. If the car is locked and the keys are lost, we first verify ownership, then open the vehicle with non-destructive methods when possible and continue with replacement and programming right there on-site.
