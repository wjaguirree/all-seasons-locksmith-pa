What should I have ready before an emergency locksmith comes to Boiling Springs?

Have your exact location, the kind of lockout, and the lock or vehicle details ready. For cars, include make, model, year, and whether the key is transponder, laser-cut, or smart/proximity. For homes or businesses, say whether the lock is a deadbolt, knob lock, or commercial lever, and whether it has been rekeyed before.

Can a locksmith open my car without damaging it?

Often, yes. A locksmith may start with non-destructive entry methods first, such as lock picking, decoding, or working the latch through the door or frame. The outcome depends on the vehicle's security design, the lock condition, and whether the car uses a standard mechanical key, transponder key, or smart system.

Can you make a new car key if I lost the only one?

In many cases, yes. A locksmith can sometimes originate a key from the VIN, cut it, and then program it to the vehicle. The exact steps depend on the make and model, especially for Toyota, Honda, Ford, Chevrolet, Nissan, Hyundai, Kia, Jeep, RAM, Subaru, and BMW systems that use transponder or proximity keys.

What makes a broken key extraction job harder?

A key becomes harder to remove when it breaks flush inside the cylinder, the lock is worn, or the fragment is pushed deeper by earlier attempts. The locksmith may use extractors, lubrication, decoding tools, or sometimes disassemble the cylinder. High-security locks like Medeco or Mul-T-Lock can require a more careful approach.
