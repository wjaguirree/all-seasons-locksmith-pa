What does an emergency locksmith check first on arrival in Hershey?

The first check is the type of lock problem and the hardware involved. A car lockout, house lockout, business lockout, broken key, or safe opening each calls for a different method. The tech looks at the lock brand, key type, and whether non-destructive entry is possible before choosing picks, decoding, bypass, extraction, or key origination.

Can you replace a lost car key if I only have the vehicle and no spare?

Yes, in many cases. The process usually starts with the vehicle identification, key style, and security system. For many makes like Toyota, Honda, Ford, Chevrolet, Nissan, Hyundai, Kia, Jeep, RAM, Subaru, and BMW, a technician may use VIN-based key origination and then program a transponder or smart key with a key programmer.

How do you open a locked house or business without damaging the door?

A good opening starts with the cylinder, not the door. The technician checks the deadbolt or knob lock, the brand, and whether the latch can be picked or bypassed without drilling. If the lock is a high-security model like Medeco or Mul-T-Lock, the method changes. The goal is to preserve the hardware whenever the condition of the lock allows it.

What if my key broke off in the lock or ignition?

Broken key extraction depends on how much of the blade remains visible and whether the cylinder is still aligned. The technician may need to pick the lock first, then use extraction tools to remove the fragment without driving it deeper. If the key snapped because the lock is worn or binding, rekeying or replacing the cylinder may be the better fix.
