What broken key extraction actually involves

Discovering a broken key lodged in your lock can turn an everyday moment into an immediate crisis. In Hummelstown, it might happen right when you're heading to the Historic Hummelstown Square or after a stroll along Swatara Creek. The first step we take is to assess the situation. Our technician will arrive fully equipped to handle both standard and high-security locks, as well as residential and commercial setups. We use specialized tools designed for key extraction, such as lock picks and extraction kits, ensuring we can retrieve the snapped key with minimal damage to the lock itself.

Key extraction often includes two main phases: removal and access. Initially, we assess how much of the key remains in the lock and whether additional tools, like a magnet or extraction pliers, are required. Non-destructive techniques are ideal for this, as they preserve the integrity of your lock. After successfully extracting the broken piece, we can create a replacement key using techniques like decoding, programmed for transponder keys or laser-cut types if needed. It's essential to have the lock's brand and key type ready to speed up the process; common brands in these areas include Kwikset and Schlage.

What tools do locksmiths use for broken key extraction?

Locksmiths typically use extraction tools such as a broken key extractor, tweezers, or a magnet. These help to safely remove the key fragment from the lock with minimal disruption. Special picks designed for non-destructive entry can also be employed, particularly in high-security locks.

How long does it take to extract a broken key?

The time it takes to extract a broken key can vary based on the lock type and the key's position. Generally, most extractions can be completed in a single visit, as our technicians come fully equipped and prepared for various situations that may arise.

Can you help if my key broke in my car lock?

Yes! Our mobile service can assist with broken key extractions from vehicles across Hummelstown. We work with various car makes, including Toyota, Honda, and Ford, and have the tools necessary to address the specific locking mechanisms used in each.

What should I do if my key is stuck in the lock?

First, avoid attempting to force it out, as this can cause further damage. Instead, give us a call, and we will send a qualified technician to assess the situation and safely extract the broken key. Having your lock brand information can help us prepare effectively.
