What should I do first if I'm locked out in Middletown, PA?

Stay with the vehicle or building if it's safe, and call with the exact location and lock type if you know it. Can you make a new car key if I lost the only one?

Yes, in many cases. The process can include VIN-based key origination, cutting the blade to code, and programming a transponder or smart key so the car recognizes it. Toyota, Honda, Ford, Chevrolet, Nissan, Hyundai, Kia, Jeep, RAM, Subaru, and BMW may each require a different procedure depending on the year and security system.

How do you open a house or business without damaging the lock?

A locksmith will usually inspect the lock body, cylinder, and door alignment first, then try non-destructive entry methods such as picking or decoding if the hardware allows it. Kwikset, Schlage, Baldwin, Yale, Medeco, and Mul-T-Lock all behave differently. If the lock is damaged, binding, or already forced, entry may require repair or replacement after access is gained.

What makes a broken key extraction harder?

A clean break in a standard cylinder is usually simpler than a key snapped deep in a worn or misaligned lock. Rust, bent keys, debris, and older hardware can all make the job tougher. If part of the key is still sticking out, extraction may be easier. If the lock is a high-security model, the technician may need special tools and a more careful process.
