Good to know

Common questions.

Can you make a car key if I lost every key in Hershey?

Yes, in many cases a replacement key can be created from the vehicle's VIN, the lock code, or by decoding the door or ignition. The exact process depends on the make and model. Transponder and smart keys also need programming so the car recognizes the new key before it will start.

What's the difference between a transponder key and a smart key?

A transponder key has a chip inside the head or shell that communicates with the immobilizer when you start the car. A smart or proximity key usually lets you unlock and start the vehicle without inserting a blade in the ignition. Both may need programming, but the equipment and steps are different.

Can a locksmith open my car without damaging it?

In many lockout cases, yes. Non-destructive entry tools are used to reach the lock mechanism or access point without forcing the door. The technique depends on the vehicle's design, the trim, the type of lock, and whether the keys are locked inside or the key itself is missing.

Why won't my key turn in the ignition anymore?

Common causes include wear inside the ignition cylinder, a bent or worn key, debris in the lock, or internal wafer damage. On some vehicles, a failing transponder chip can look like an ignition problem because the car won't start even though the key turns. Diagnosis comes first so the right repair is chosen.
