Common questions.

What should I have ready before calling for car key replacement in Highspire?

Have the vehicle year, make, and model ready, plus proof of ownership and your location in Highspire. If you still have one working key or fob, bring it. Knowing whether the vehicle uses a metal key, transponder key, laser-cut key, or smart fob helps us bring the right blanks and programming tools.

Can you make a new key if I lost every copy?

Yes, in many cases we can originate a new key from the vehicle itself or from the VIN when the system allows it. The process may include decoding, cutting a fresh key, and programming a transponder or smart key so the car recognizes it. Some vehicles also need security authorization before programming will work.

What is the difference between a transponder key and a smart key?

A transponder key has a chip inside the head of the key that must be recognized by the immobilizer before the engine starts. A smart or proximity key communicates with the vehicle wirelessly, often allowing push-button start and passive entry. Each type needs different equipment for cutting, programming, and testing.

Why would my ignition need repair instead of just a new key?

If the key turns hard, sticks, or only works when wiggled, the issue may be inside the ignition cylinder. Worn wafers, damaged pins, or a bent key can cause that. In those cases, a new key alone may not solve it. We inspect the cylinder, compare wear to the key cut, and decide whether repair or replacement makes sense.
