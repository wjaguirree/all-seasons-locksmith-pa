Common questions.

Can you make a car key in Schaefferstown if I lost every copy?

Yes. If the vehicle allows it, a locksmith can originate a new key using the VIN, lock decoding, or key-code information, then program the transponder or fob so the car recognizes it. The exact path depends on the make, model, year, and whether the immobilizer or ignition has been changed before.

What is the difference between a transponder key and a smart fob?

A transponder key has a chip that communicates with the immobilizer when the blade turns in the ignition. A smart or proximity fob can let the vehicle unlock and start without a traditional key turn, depending on the system. Both usually require programming, but the tools and procedure are different.

Why would my ignition turn but not start the car?

That can point to a worn key, a damaged ignition cylinder, or an anti-theft issue. If the key is too worn, the wafers may not line up correctly. If the cylinder is damaged, the key may turn but not engage properly. On many vehicles, the immobilizer also needs to be checked before assuming the ignition itself failed.

Do you work on Toyota, Ford, Honda, Chevrolet, and Jeep keys?

Yes, those are common automotive platforms for key replacement, duplication, and programming. The exact service depends on whether the vehicle uses a standard metal key, a transponder key, a laser-cut key, or a smart fob. Some models also require specific key-programming steps or security access before a new key will start the vehicle.

I'm out on a farm lane near Route 419 with the key locked in the truck. What should I do first?

Call with the vehicle make, model, year, and your exact location details, then stay with the truck if it is safe to do so. If you have proof of ownership handy, that helps the service go smoothly.
