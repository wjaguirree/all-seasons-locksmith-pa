What lock rekeying actually involves

In Central Pennsylvania, a house can go from quiet to busy fast: spring turnover for student rentals, a winter move into an older place near the square, or a wet stretch where people are carrying in boxes and never sure who still has an old key. Lock rekeying solves that without changing the hardware. We keep the knob, deadbolt, or handle set in place and change the pins inside the cylinder so the old keys stop working and a new key takes over.

The process starts with identifying the lock brand and cylinder style. Kwikset and Schlage are common on homes in and around Carlisle, but Baldwin, Yale, Medeco, and Mul-T-Lock show up too, especially on upgraded doors. We remove the cylinder, read the pin stack, and repin it with a fresh key code using a pinning kit. If a lock is worn, keyed differently on each side, or damaged from a sticky winter door, that can affect the work. For many homes, we can rekey multiple locks to one key so front, back, and side entries all match. It is a good fit for new homeowners, landlords turning over a student rental near Dickinson College, or anyone who wants to reset access after a move near High Street, Route 11, or the historic square.

We also check whether the door hardware is worth keeping. Many residential locks are ANSI Grade 2 or Grade 3, which are fine to rekey if the internals are still sound. If a cylinder is pinned out of order, the keyway is damaged, or the lock has been forced, rekeying may not be enough. In those cases, the job becomes part locksmithing and part troubleshooting: clean the cylinder, test the plug, and make sure the new key turns smoothly before we leave.

Good to know

Common questions.

What does lock rekeying change?

Rekeying changes the pins inside the lock cylinder so the old key no longer works. The outside hardware stays in place. It is the right choice when you want to control access after a move, a tenant change, or a lost key, without replacing a perfectly usable lock.

Can you rekey all the locks on my Carlisle home to one key?

Often, yes. If the locks are compatible, we can set the front door, back door, and other exterior locks to one key. The tech checks the brand, cylinder type, and keyway first. Older homes around High Street or the historic square sometimes have mixed hardware, which may limit what can be matched.

Do you need the original key to rekey a lock?

No, not always. A locksmith can often rekey a lock without the original key by removing the cylinder and repinning it to a new key. If the lock is badly worn, damaged, or missing parts, that can change the approach. The cylinder has to be serviceable for the work to hold up.

What kinds of locks can be rekeyed?

Most residential knob locks, lever locks, and deadbolts from brands like Kwikset, Schlage, Baldwin, and many Yale setups can be rekeyed. Some Medeco and Mul-T-Lock hardware also has rekeyable cylinders, but the process can be more involved. A locksmith checks the exact model before starting.

Is rekeying a good idea for student rentals near Dickinson College?

Yes. Student turnover is a strong reason to rekey because keys can circulate quickly and access needs to change between leases. In Carlisle, that is common around Dickinson College and the U.S. Army War College as well as older rentals near the historic square. Rekeying resets who can enter without changing the door hardware.
