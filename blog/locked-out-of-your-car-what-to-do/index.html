Locked Out? What to Do for Car, House & Business Lockouts

Locked out right now? Stay put somewhere safe. Don't force the door. Call a mobile locksmith. All Seasons Locksmith handles car, house, and business lockouts across 31 Central PA towns out of Harrisburg, and (223) 240-3505 gets us headed your way.

Don't panic. And don't force anything.

Most people's instinct is to start yanking on the door or poking at the lock. Stop. Parking lot? Get somewhere visible, away from traffic. House or business lockout after dark? Find a well-lit spot, ideally with someone nearby.

Then check the easy stuff first. A back door that's unlocked. A spare you gave your sister two years ago and forgot about. That fake rock by the porch. Nothing? Okay, time to call.

Coat hangers and flathead screwdrivers work great in movies. In real life they wreck doors. Weatherstripping, window motors, even airbag sensors sit closer to a car's door edge than most people realize, and on a house or storefront, prying at the jamb cracks the frame. A five-minute lockout turns into a repair bill nobody wanted. Non-destructive tools, built for the actual lock, get you in clean. No marks, no cracked jamb, no scratched paint.

Car lockouts

Keys locked inside. Keys lost. A key that's just stopped working. A mobile automotive locksmith handles all three the same visit. Most cars open without a scratch, and a new key or fob usually gets cut and programmed right there off your VIN. No tow, no dealership trip, no waiting a week for a part. Full details on our car lockout service.

Snapped a key off in the door or ignition? Leave the tweezers in the drawer. Digging at it just pushes the piece deeper and scratches the cylinder on the way. Extraction needs the right tool for the job, and once that fragment's out, we can usually cut a replacement before we leave.

House lockouts

A door that auto-locks behind you, a lost key, a lock that's simply worn out and sticking, it's the same problem in a different frame. Most standard deadbolts and knob locks open without drilling, and we'll cut a spare while we're already there. Lost a key rather than just misplaced it? That's your cue to rekey, not just re-lock. Full guide here: when to rekey your home's locks.

Business lockout? That one's different.

Every hour a storefront or office sits closed is money walking past the door. Standard hardware, panic bars, most master key systems, a commercial locksmith gets them open without tearing anything up, and usually rekeys or replaces the affected lock the same day so it's not a repeat call next month. If it keeps happening because half the staff carries a key that opens everything, that's not a lock problem. It's a master key system problem, and the fix is different.

What actually happens when we show up

We confirm the address, quote you by phone before anyone touches a tool, no surprises later. On site, the technician sizes up the lock or the vehicle and reaches for whatever non-destructive tool fits it. Opening the door, cutting a key, pulling a broken fragment, sometimes it's all three in one stop. Insured, and we run all 31 towns across Dauphin, Cumberland, and Lebanon counties.
